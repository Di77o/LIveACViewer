# LG product reference images

These product reference images were downloaded from LG Electronics’ public LG
Klimaat regional website on 3 November 2026. They are manufacturer photographs,
not original artwork licensed under this repository’s software license. LG and
ARTCOOL/DUALCOOL names and product artwork belong to their respective owners.

The exact source URL for each asset is recorded in `src/data/catalog.ts`.
Images remain as served by LG; the Matt Black and Deluxe images use LG’s
documented website image-size query parameter to limit download size.

Dimensions and cooling capacities are transcribed from pages 4–5 of the official
[LG 2026 RAC brochure](https://www.lgklimaat.nl/media/qlypkfv5/lg_rac-flyer-digital-airco_nl_2026.pdf).
Belgian family listings are linked from each catalog entry. Matt Black is listed
on the Netherlands site, so Belgian availability remains unverified.

This collection is a starter catalog rather than a complete inventory of Belgian
SKUs. Installer confirmation is required for current availability, the exact
capacity variant, installation clearances, and mounting dimensions.
